# License decision

**Decision date:** 2026-08-08

**Decision:** no project-level license; keep the repository private.

## Why no license was added

The local files identify the work as the Healthcare Recovery Module for Innoverse AI Challenge #17. The following material facts are not documented in the project or the available official 2026 guide:

- the exact challenge #17 submission and publication terms;
- the team roster and any contributor or employer assignment;
- ownership of the original submission and later changes;
- the source, license, and redistribution permission for the supplied CSV; and
- permission to publish or commercialize before or after judging.

Selecting MIT, Apache-2.0, GPL, a proprietary notice, or any other project license would therefore overstate authority. GitHub's licensing guidance also explains that a repository without a license is not open source and that default copyright rules apply; it separately warns that public GitHub repositories can still be viewed and forked under GitHub's terms.

## Current treatment

- No `LICENSE` file is present.
- The remote repository must remain private.
- No exclusive copyright-owner statement is made.
- The supplied dataset is ignored and must not be uploaded.
- Chart.js remains covered by its independent MIT license and notice.
- Contributions are paused until the maintainer can identify the rights needed to accept and license them.

## Clearance needed before changing this decision

Obtain written evidence for every item in [PUBLICATION_GATE.md](PUBLICATION_GATE.md). Then identify the correct rights holders, preserve required attribution, confirm organizer and dataset obligations, and choose only a license that those rights holders are authorized to grant.

This is a conservative engineering/publication decision, not legal advice.
