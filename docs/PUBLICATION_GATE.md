# Publication gate

**Status:** blocked for public release as of 2026-08-08. A private repository and private prerelease are permitted for controlled engineering review.

## Evidence found

- The product interface identifies “Healthcare Recovery Module — Innoverse AI Challenge #17.”
- The [official Innoverse site](https://innoverse.world/) lists 2026 registration closing June 10, judging on August 15–22, and awards on August 24.
- The [official 2026 guide book](https://innoverse.world/innoverse%202026%20guide%20book.pdf) describes event and submission logistics.
- The supplied CSV contains operational-looking fields but no embedded source, license, data card, or redistribution notice. It appears structurally generated, but appearance is not proof of provenance or rights.
- No local team agreement, contributor assignment, submission receipt, challenge #17 brief, organizer permission, or dataset license was found.

The reviewed official materials did not establish a public-source license, dataset redistribution license, ownership allocation, embargo, or post-submission publication right for this project.

## Written clearance required

Before changing the repository to public or publishing release materials, obtain and retain:

1. The exact 2026 Innoverse AI Challenge #17 brief and binding participation/submission terms.
2. Organizer confirmation of repository visibility, judging embargo, post-event publication, attribution, branding, and commercialization rules.
3. The complete team/contributor roster and written confirmation of each contributor's ownership or assignment position.
4. Any employer, school, sponsor, client, or platform agreement that could control the work.
5. The supplied dataset's authoritative source, owner, license, permitted purpose, and redistribution terms.
6. Confirmation that screenshots and derived metrics may be published.
7. The verified rights holders' authorization for the selected project license.

## Current controls

- Keep the GitHub repository private.
- Do not publish GitHub Pages, a public demo, public release assets, or competition promotional posts.
- Do not commit either the supplied CSV or the generated demo CSV.
- Do not claim an award, official recognition, organizer endorsement, exclusive ownership, clinical efficacy, or real-hospital deployment.
- Preserve neutral AIONEX maintainer branding without representing AIONEX as a verified legal entity or sole rights holder.
- Keep the release marked as a private prerelease.

## Re-evaluation checklist

When the evidence is available, record document titles, effective dates, URLs or copies, and the exact provisions governing code, data, publication, attribution, team rights, and commercialization. Update `NOTICE.md`, `docs/LICENSE_DECISION.md`, contributor guidance, screenshots, README wording, repository visibility, and release notes as one reviewed change.

This checklist is not legal advice. Escalate conflicting or ambiguous terms to qualified counsel or the organizer in writing.
