# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Project collaboration should be professional, patient, and safe for people of
different backgrounds and levels of experience. Participants are expected to:

- discuss ideas and evidence without personal attacks;
- use inclusive, precise language and respect names, identities, and privacy;
- give actionable feedback and accept good-faith correction;
- avoid harassment, threats, sexualized attention, discrimination, doxxing, or
  sustained disruption;
- protect credentials, personal data, restricted competition information, and
  vulnerability details; and
- avoid overstating the prototype's healthcare, safety, legal, or production
  capabilities.

These expectations apply in issues, reviews, commits, project discussions,
demonstrations, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Reporting concerns

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository administrator through the
private communication channel associated with repository access. Do not place
personal, security-sensitive, or retaliation-risk details in an issue. A
security vulnerability should instead follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Include the relevant dates, location, participants, what happened, and any
supporting material you are authorized to share. Reports will be limited to the
people needed to assess and respond, subject to platform and legal obligations.
Confidentiality cannot be guaranteed where disclosure is required for safety or
lawful process.

## Response

Repository administrators may clarify expectations, remove content, restrict
participation, revoke access, or refer a matter to the hosting platform or an
appropriate authority. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior,
impact, context, and pattern of conduct. Good-faith reports and appeals will be
considered without retaliation from project maintainers.
